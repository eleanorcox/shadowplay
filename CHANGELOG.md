# Changelog

What changed in each version of Shadowplay, as short lists. For what each version was like to use, and its screencast, see the [version log](docs/versions.md).

Versions are numbered v1, v2 and so on, each with a git tag of the same name. Newest first.

## Unreleased

- Six more puppets, making nine. Animals: the spider (hand hanging down, two eyes) and the butterfly (two hands, thumbs hooked; it grows feelers). Things: the walker (two fingers walking; it becomes a little person with a head), the candle (a flame lights on your fingertip), the tree (leaves grow on your fingers) and the heart (two hands; it glows and beats).
- Two hands making a puppet together cast one shadow and share one hint.
- The cards shrink to fit all nine across the wall, and the demonstration plays through every puppet.
- Folding a finger no longer counts as spreading it, so a half-made rabbit is not mistaken for a tree.

## v1: the first wall (18 November 2026)

- A lamp-lit wall where the shadows of your hands appear, drawn from the hand points; the camera picture is never shown.
- Three puppets, recognised for either hand: rabbit, duck and dog. Hold one and it comes alive: its eye opens and blinks, and the duck quacks and the dog barks as you open the beak or mouth.
- Cards along the bottom show how to hold your hand and the shadow it makes; the one you are nearest lights up with a closeness bar, and each puppet you make gets a star.
- Kind hints for the puppet you are nearest to, such as "Touch your thumb to your folded fingers".
- When nobody is there, a demonstration hand plays through the puppets, with "Hold up a hand in the light"; the stars clear for the next visitors.
- `?demo` and `?demo=rabbit` (or `duck`, `dog`, `open`) run without a camera.
- The camera reconnects by itself if it fails or is unplugged.
