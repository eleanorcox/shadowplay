Decisions that shape the plan. Roughly in order of how much they change it.

1. **Where would it be shown, and how big?** A screen on a stand, a projected wall, a table? It decides the camera position (children's hands are lower) and when the projector stage matters.
2. **Hand-drawn art?** The puppet cards are drawn from the model hand for now ([decision 8](decisions.md#8-a-model-hand-draws-the-lessons-and-the-demo)). Would Eleanor like to draw them, or the shadows' scenery, later?
3. **Sound?** Galleries are often shared, quiet spaces. Proposed: sound off by default, a setting for when the space suits it.
